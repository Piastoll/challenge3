How to tell them apart

  • parser: day and month swap places (7 April arrives as 4 July). It comes from a day-first tenant through the app, email, or a booking-v2 form. The tenant’s locale decides this, not the reporter’s browser.
  • hint: the same swap, but only through the booking-v3 form of a day-first tenant. The form’s hint asks for MM/DD/YYYY, so people type the month first, and the form reads it day first. The parser fix doesn’t touch the hint. It ended when booking-v3 was retired.
  • shift: the month stays and the day moves by one (Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May, and Lisbon and Toronto get different days for the same meeting). A swap can’t do that. Look at time-zone and DST handling when invites are sent.
